Student Stress & Mental Health

Excel · Tableau

Which factors are most associated with student stress, and what would a school actually do with that information?

1,100student records
21variables analyzed
55%anxiety increase, low to high bullying
5recommendations for schools
  • Bullying is linked to a 55% increase in anxiety and a 33% drop in self-esteem between the least- and most-bullied groups — though the pattern isn't perfectly linear, so I'm treating it as an association, not proof.
  • High-stress students show a 2.83-point drop in sleep quality, the single largest lifestyle swing anywhere in the dataset.
  • One mental-health-score group breaks the pattern entirely — the lowest study load in the dataset, but still weak academic performance. Flagged as an open question rather than an assumption about motivation.

Record Label Performance in Spotify's Most-Streamed Songs

SQLite

Which record label is actually running the smartest business — and how much of its lead is new hits versus old catalogue?

4,600tracks analyzed
1,999unique artists
58artists mapped to labels
5business questions
  • Universal leads on total streams, but 61.8% of that comes from pre-2020 catalogue — a lot of the lead is legacy, not new hits.
  • Independent artists convert TikTok attention into Spotify streams at 285.94% — more than five times Sony's rate of 53.5%, though on a sample of just two artists.
  • Avicii still sits at 178% of Universal's per-artist average, six years after his death — one of the clearer signals of how long a strong catalogue keeps earning.
