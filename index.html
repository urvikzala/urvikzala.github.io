Student Stress & Mental Health

Excel · Tableau

Which factors are most associated with student stress, and what would a school actually do with that information?

1,100student records
21variables in the source dataset
55%anxiety increase, low to high bullying
5recommendations for schools
  • Bullying is linked to a 55% increase in anxiety and a 33% drop in self-esteem between the least- and most-bullied groups. The pattern isn't perfectly linear, though, so I'm treating it as an association, not proof.
  • High-stress students show a 2.83-point drop in sleep quality, the largest change among the four lifestyle measures compared.
  • One mental-health-score group breaks the pattern entirely: the lowest study load in the dataset, but still weak academic performance. Flagged as an open question rather than an assumption about motivation.

Record Label Performance in Spotify's Most-Streamed Songs

SQLite

Which record label is actually running the smartest business, and how much of its lead is new hits versus old catalogue?

4,600tracks in the source dataset
1,999unique artists
58artists mapped to labels
5business questions
  • Universal leads on total streams, but 61.8% of that comes from pre-2020 catalogue, meaning a lot of the lead is legacy, not new hits.
  • Independent artists show the highest Spotify-to-TikTok activity ratio at 285.94%, though the result is based on only two mapped artists and does not measure conversion.
  • Avicii's average streams per song reached 178% of Universal's per-song average in the 2024 dataset, demonstrating the staying power of a strong catalogue.
